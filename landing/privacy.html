Privacy notice

What we collect when you request the demo, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Wayscope. Contact: JDavis@wayscope.co.uk.

What we collect from the demo form

Your name, work email, organisation, role, the message you type, whether you ticked "product updates", and the date and time of the request.

Why

To send you the demo access and follow up on your request (our legitimate interest in responding to someone who asked to see the product). If you ticked "product updates", to send occasional updates about Wayscope; you can stop these at any time by replying "stop".

Where it's kept

In the Wayscope application database, hosted by Render, and in our Google Workspace email. We don't sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long

Up to 24 months from your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, a correction or deletion by emailing JDavis@wayscope.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

The demo itself

The demo is a fictional scheme and the access details are shared between everyone viewing it, so please don't enter real project data. This site sets no tracking cookies.

* The show week (12–18 Oct) and the week after are populated for every approved line.
* "FICTIONAL DEMO SCHEME · NOTHING HERE IS REAL": a new tenant setting (tenant.demo_notice)
  shown as an amber pill in the header on every screen, as a pill on the public map, as a

WHAT YOU DO, IN ORDER (the Codespace, then the site)
----------------------------------------------------
1. Apply the zip (commands below) and let Render deploy (~2 min).

2. RE-IMPORT THE DEMO. In the Codespace terminal (it has the repo and curl). replace=1
   wipes the current demo tenant's rows first — it is the demo, nothing else is on it:

     curl -X POST "https://wayscope.co.uk/api/admin/tenant/import?replace=1&token=PASTE_YOUR_ADMIN_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" --data-binary @demo/uk-corridor.package.json

   Expect: {"ok":true,"inserted":{...,"location_gates":14,"zones":5,"route_haul_roads":6,
   "forecasts":74,...},"replaced":true,...}. Then press Enter once more and clear the
   terminal (the token is on screen).

3. BAKE. Hard-refresh the site, sign in as Admin, Routes page: in the route form tick
   THREE vehicles — Artic Tipper (44t), Artic Flatbed (44t), Rigid 8-wheeler (32t) — then
   "Bake all · 3 vehicle(s)" with both legs. ~220 HERE calls, inside the free tier. Wait
   for it to finish (the status counts routes). The Dashboard should then read 72 of 72
   line-months baked.

4. Look-ahead → "check now" once (National Highways; seconds). Then "next week": six real
   ROADWORKS flags (the A46 overnight closures on 15–17 Oct) plus the demo's own flags.

5. Walk the three stories once before the stand:
   JV / corridor  — Dashboard, teams and sections on the Route map, the stockpile at Tilton.
   Council        — the public map signed out, Burrough village 7.5 t, the caps on R016/R018
                    (ROUTE_CAP), the Look-ahead PDF as the weekly note.
   Urban          — Harborough Compound's two gates on the Routes map, the A47 closure
                    re-route (Zones page: toggle "affects routing" off → re-bake offer →
                    on again to show the before/after), the Pending line on Forecasts.
